About

I design and make jewellery and objects that sit somewhere between industrial precision and the hand. My background is in industrial design, followed by a year of apprenticeship at Elird Studio, where I learned to work at the bench. That combination shapes how I work. I think in structure, proportion and material behaviour, then I test everything by making it. Nothing leaves the studio as a rendering. Through doba.objects, the brand I founded in Sarajevo, I develop pieces that are quiet, deliberate and built to be worn rather than displayed. Alongside my own collections I have worked across costume, set and interior design, which taught me to design for context, deadline and constraint as much as for form. I take on commissions, collaborations and studio work.
